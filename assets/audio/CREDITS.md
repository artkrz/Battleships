# Audio sources

Thimras — Battle at sea, CC0 1.0:
https://opengameart.org/content/battle-at-sea
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source WAVs are lossless decodes of:
- destruction.wav: ship_destroyed_short.ogg
- impact.wav: cannon_hit_ship_short.ogg
- splash.wav: cannon_miss_1.ogg

Game cues are edited excerpts, trimmed, faded and level matched using
scripts/generate-audio.py. Sunk mixes destruction with a short water splash.
No additional reverb, synthesized drones or musical tones are used.
